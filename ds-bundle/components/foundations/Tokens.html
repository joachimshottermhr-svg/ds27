Known gaps - what is deliberately NOT in this layer

Every one of these is a value that could not be verified or does not exist in the V27 export. None has been invented. A stated gap is useful; a plausible invention is not.

GapWhat is missing
Chart palettechart-1 is the only chart token and it is #ffffff in both modes. A real three-colour palette (#0075be, #fc8700, #00ad60) exists but is not in the V27 export.#3, #25
Shadow tierNone. Toast and Step carry shadows whose colour is Border/Border - Drop shadow, not a V27 variable.#19
Orange 50Every other ramp has ten steps; Orange has nine. No tenth was interpolated.#5
Three heading stylesXL Heading, Larger Heading and Sub heading are named on the style guide but node 1:27 exceeds the metadata ceiling, so they could not be read off their nodes.#1, #11
AI gradientThe AI gradient style serialises empty - gradients do not come through this toolchain. Exposed as --v27-ai-accent with a neutral fallback rather than a guessed brand colour.#32
OutfitReferenced with a system fallback. Not licensed, not bundled - the library renders in the fallback unless the app loads it.#9
Off-scale literalsThe Button's 42px height and 207px radius, the circle icon's 35px radius, Toast's 11px gap and others sit on no scale. They stay literals with their node ids rather than being rounded onto one.#10, #13

Seven variables bound on V27 components come from the People First library and are absent from the V27 export entirely - Text/Text - Primary, Icons/Icon - Required field, Border/Border - Drop shadow, Border/Secondary, Charts/Chart 1-3, 2nd (light)/Grey steel and a style named DEPRECATED COLOURS/White. The V27 export alone cannot rebuild the V27 file. See FINDINGS.md #18 and #28.
